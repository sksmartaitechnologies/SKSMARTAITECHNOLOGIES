Internship Roadmap & Deliverables

A guided sequence makes progress visible from initial setup through final presentation.

  1. 01

    Week 1–2: Foundation

    Set up tools, agree on goals and practice the fundamentals needed for the assigned role.

    Deliverable: learning plan, environment and starter exercises.
  2. 02

    Week 3–6: Live Client Sprint

    Work through a scoped team brief with mentor check-ins, reviews and clear acceptance criteria.

    Deliverable: reviewed sprint output and progress demo.
  3. 03

    Final Capstone & Certification

    Refine and present a portfolio-ready capstone with documentation and a completion review.

    Deliverable: final presentation and verified certification, subject to completion.
